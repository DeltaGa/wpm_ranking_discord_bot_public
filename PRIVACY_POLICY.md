# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** June 26, 2025  
**Last Updated:** June 26, 2025

## Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how the Advanced WPM Ranking Discord Bot ("wpm_ranking", "the Bot," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our Discord bot service. By using the Bot, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## Information We Collect

### Automatically Collected Data
- **Discord User ID**: Unique identifier provided by Discord
- **Username**: Your Discord display name for leaderboards and statistics
- **Server/Guild ID**: Identifier of Discord servers where the Bot is used
- **Message Content**: Only when explicitly interacting with Bot commands
- **Timestamps**: When you use Bot features and commands

### Reading Performance Data
- **Reading Speed (WPM)**: Words per minute measurements from tests
- **Accuracy Scores**: Comprehension test results and percentages
- **Session Duration**: Time spent on reading tests
- **Text Complexity**: Difficulty levels of materials you've read
- **Language Preferences**: Your selected reading languages
- **Progress Statistics**: Historical performance data and trends

### AI Interaction Data
- **Comprehension Responses**: Your answers to AI-generated questions
- **Question Generation Logs**: Metadata about AI-generated content (not personal)
- **Model Performance**: Anonymous effectiveness metrics of AI systems
- **Processing Times**: Technical performance data for system optimization

### File Upload Data
- **File Metadata**: Name, size, format of uploaded documents
- **Extracted Text**: Content analysis for reading metrics (temporarily processed)
- **Analysis Results**: Readability scores, word counts, complexity ratings
- **Upload Frequency**: Daily usage tracking for rate limiting

### User Preferences
- **Language Settings**: Preferred reading languages and exclusivity preferences
- **Bot Configuration**: Personal settings and feature preferences
- **Achievement Progress**: Badges earned and milestone tracking

## How We Use Your Information

### Core Functionality
- **Performance Tracking**: Calculate reading speeds, accuracy, and improvement trends
- **Ranking Systems**: Generate leaderboards and competitive rankings
- **Achievement Systems**: Track progress and award badges
- **Personalized Experience**: Tailor content and recommendations to your skill level

### AI-Powered Features
- **Question Generation**: Create comprehension tests using local AI models
- **Content Analysis**: Evaluate text complexity and readability
- **Performance Optimization**: Improve AI model effectiveness
- **Fallback Systems**: Provide manual alternatives when AI is unavailable

### Analytics and Improvement
- **Usage Statistics**: Understand feature popularity and effectiveness
- **Performance Monitoring**: Ensure system reliability and speed
- **Bug Detection**: Identify and resolve technical issues
- **Feature Development**: Improve existing features and develop new ones

### Communication
- **Support Responses**: Address user questions and technical issues
- **System Notifications**: Inform about updates, maintenance, or important changes
- **Community Features**: Enable leaderboards, challenges, and social features

## Data Processing and Storage

### Local Processing
- **AI Operations**: All AI processing occurs locally using Ollama (no external AI APIs)
- **File Analysis**: Document processing happens on our servers, not third-party services
- **Comprehension Generation**: Question creation uses local language models only

### Data Storage
- **Database Location**: SQLite database stored on secure servers
- **Backup Procedures**: Regular automated backups with encryption
- **Retention Period**: Data retained indefinitely unless user requests deletion
- **Access Controls**: Restricted access with authentication and authorization

### Data Security
- **Encryption**: Data encrypted in transit and at rest where technically feasible
- **Access Logging**: All data access attempts are logged and monitored
- **Regular Updates**: Security patches applied promptly
- **Incident Response**: Established procedures for potential security breaches

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

### We DO NOT Share Your Data With:
- **Third-Party AI Services**: All AI processing is local (Ollama)
- **Analytics Companies**: No external analytics services receive your data
- **Marketing Companies**: We do not sell or rent your information
- **Data Brokers**: Your information is never sold to third parties

### Limited Disclosure Scenarios
We may disclose your information only in these specific circumstances:

- **Legal Compliance**: When required by law, court order, or government regulation
- **Safety Protection**: To protect the safety of users or prevent illegal activities
- **Service Providers**: To essential service providers under strict confidentiality agreements
- **Business Transfer**: In the event of merger, acquisition, or asset sale (users will be notified)

### Discord Integration
- **Discord Terms**: Your use is also governed by Discord's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service
- **Public Information**: Leaderboard rankings may be visible to other server members
- **Server Administrators**: Server owners can see usage statistics for their servers

## Your Rights and Controls

### Data Access and Portability
- **View Your Data**: Use `/mystats` command to see your stored information
- **Export Data**: Request a complete export of your data via `/feedback`
- **Data Format**: Exported data provided in machine-readable format (JSON)

### Data Correction and Updates
- **Username Updates**: Automatically synced with Discord username changes
- **Preference Changes**: Modify language and feature preferences anytime
- **Error Correction**: Report data inaccuracies via `/feedback` command

### Data Deletion Rights
- **Complete Deletion**: Use `/reset` command to delete all your data permanently
- **Partial Deletion**: Request specific data deletion via `/feedback`
- **Account Deactivation**: Remove the Bot from your servers to stop data collection
- **Retention Override**: Data deleted within 30 days of verified deletion request

### Privacy Controls
- **Language Preferences**: Control which languages appear in your tests
- **File Analysis**: Choose whether to use file analysis features
- **AI Features**: Opt-out of AI-powered comprehension tests
- **Public Visibility**: Control leaderboard appearance (where technically possible)
